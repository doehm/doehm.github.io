Datasets

Four tidy tables that join on version, season and id. Each is also available as its own CSV (UTF-8).

DatasetDescriptionRowsColumnsCSV
survivalistsContains details of each survivalist including demographics and results.18020survivalists.csv (31 KB)
loadoutsInformation on each survivalists loadout of 10 items1,3507loadouts.csv (81 KB)
episodesContains details of each episode including the title, number of viewers, beginning quote and IMDb rating19514episodes.csv (49 KB)
